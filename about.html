About UniApplySA

Helping students across all provinces of South Africa

Our Mission

UniApplySA was built to help Grade 12 learners from all provinces apply to universities across South Africa in one place.

No matter where you are in South Africa, we make applications simple. We cover all 26 public universities, NSFAS, CAO, prospectus, APS calculation and application tracking.

Built by JDev App Craft Co. to serve learners nationwide.
