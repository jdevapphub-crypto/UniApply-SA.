Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026 - UniApplySA

At UniApplySA, accessible from https://jdevapphub-crypto.github.io/UniApplySA/, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information automatically. When you use our contact form, you voluntarily provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message

We only use this to reply to your university application enquiry. We never sell your data.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

We also use browser localStorage to remember which universities you ticked as applied. This stays on your device only.

3. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to official university websites (UCT, Wits, UJ, UP, Stellenbosch, UNISA, etc) and TVET colleges, as well as NSFAS. Clicking those links will take you to external sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please check their own privacy policies.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your messages via email or WhatsApp (073 172 5505)
  • To improve our free application guide
  • To prevent spam and abuse

5. Data Protection

We do not store your contact form data on our servers. Messages are sent directly to our email uniapplysa7@gmail.com via FormSubmit, a secure form service. We delete emails after your query is resolved.

6. Children's Information

UniApplySA is intended for Grade 11, Grade 12 and post-matric students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights (POPIA - South Africa)

Under South Africa's POPI Act, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us at uniapplysa7@gmail.com to do so.

By using our website, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
